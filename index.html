FAQ

Before you book.

Do you work by appointment only?

Yes. Sully's is an appointment-only detailing studio so every vehicle receives the time and attention it needs.

Do you offer mobile detailing?

No. Sully's Detailing Studio is a dedicated studio service.

Are your prices fixed?

Listed prices are starting prices. Final pricing depends on vehicle size, condition and the exact work required.

Do you offer ceramic coatings?

Yes. Ceramic coating services include the preparation required for the selected coating.

Will you offer PPF?

PPF is planned as a future service once the installation training and studio setup are ready.
