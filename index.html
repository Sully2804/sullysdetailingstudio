03 — ABOUT SULLY

From roadside
washes to a
detailing studio.

I'm Sully — founder, detailer, materials scientist and unapologetic detailing nerd.

Long before there was a studio, there was me, a car, a bucket and an unhealthy obsession with getting paintwork absolutely right.

I started detailing cars on the side of the road. Since then, that obsession has evolved into something considerably more serious.

I hold a PhD in Materials Science, so naturally I ended up researching coatings, abrasives, surface chemistry and protection products far more than any normal person probably should.

Every product used at the studio is selected because I believe it earns its place here — not because the bottle has the best marketing.
