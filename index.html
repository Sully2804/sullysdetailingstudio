ABOUT SULLY

Meet The Detailer Behind The Studio.

I'm Sully — founder of Sully's Detailing Studio, materials scientist and product obsessive.

My journey started detailing cars on the side of the road with nothing more than a bucket, a wash mitt and an obsession with making paint look perfect.

Professionally I hold a PhD in Materials Science, which made me fascinated by coatings, abrasives, paint chemistry and surface protection long before opening a detailing studio.

Every product used inside the studio has been researched, tested and chosen because it performs — not because it has the loudest marketing.
