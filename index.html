Frequently Asked Questions

Most full ceramic coating treatments take between 2 to 3 days. This timeline allows for thorough decontamination, multi-stage paint correction, coating application, and IR lamp curing in a climate-controlled environment.

Traditional waxes lay on top of the paint surface and last 1–3 months. Nano-ceramic coatings chemically bond to your vehicle's clear coat, forming a hard, hydrophobic glass-like layer that lasts several years.

No prior washing is needed. Every package begins with our multi-step decontamination rinse, iron removal wash, and clay bar treatment to ensure an immaculate surface.
