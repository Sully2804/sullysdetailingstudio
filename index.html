ABOUT SULLY

The Detailer Behind The Studio.

I'm Sully — founder of Sully's Detailing Studio, materials scientist, car enthusiast and unapologetic detailing product nerd.

The studio started long before there was a studio. I began detailing cars on the side of the road, figuring things out one vehicle at a time and becoming slightly obsessed with making paint look ridiculously good.

Away from detailing, I hold a PhD in Materials Science. That background naturally pulled me towards the chemistry, surface science, abrasives, coatings and protection technologies behind modern detailing.

I don't buy products simply because the bottle looks good or because somebody on Instagram says it's the next big thing. I research them, compare them and obsess over how they actually perform.

Every product and process used at the studio is chosen with the same philosophy: if it doesn't perform, it doesn't belong on your car.

Now, what started at the roadside is becoming a dedicated studio built around doing things properly — with premium products, proper processes and a genuine obsession with cars.
